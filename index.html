From the first survey
to the last chair.

Design-build in the Hudson Valley. We draw it, we build it, we furnish it. One contract, one team, one site visit a week until you have the keys.

01 · Site

2.4 acres, facing south.

A gentle slope above the valley in Dutchess County. We walk every site before we draw a line: sun, wind, drainage, the trees worth keeping.

02 · Survey & excavation · weeks 1–2

Marked out, dug out.

Stakes and string first. Then the footprint is cut into the hill and the foundation trench follows the plan to the inch.

03 · Foundation · weeks 3–5

One slab, poured once.

Insulated concrete slab with radiant heat loops inside it. Anchor bolts set before the pour, so the frame lands exactly where it should.

04 · Framing · weeks 6–10

The house you can read.

2×6 stud walls, engineered joists, and the cantilever carried by steel inside the timber. Our own crew; no framing subcontractor.

05 · Dry-in · weeks 11–14

Sealed before the weather turns.

Sheathing, wrap and roof membrane. From this week the interior schedule runs regardless of rain.

06 · Windows · weeks 15–16

Forty-six feet of glass.

Thermally broken black steel units, floor to ceiling along the south face. Ordered in week 2 so they arrive the day the openings are ready.

07 · Cladding · weeks 17–22

Cedar, two ways.

Grey-washed vertical cedar on the ground floor, charred cedar on the upper volume. Both go on as a rain screen, board by board.

08 · Landscape · weeks 23–26

The ground put back.

Topsoil returned and seeded, the oak untouched, a gravel apron where the cars go. The house should look like it was always there.

09 · Move-in · week 28

Twenty-eight weeks.

Fixed schedule, fixed price, one point of contact. You see the same two faces from the first survey to the day we hand over the keys.

How we work

One contract. One team. One schedule.

Most houses are built by three companies that have never met: an architect, a general contractor, and, later, a decorator. Every handover costs weeks and money, and nobody owns the result.

We own it. Our architects, builders and interior team sit at one table from the first site walk. The drawings are priced as they are drawn, the furniture is specified as the walls go up, and the schedule is fixed before we break ground.
