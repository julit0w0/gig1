HALVORSEN DESIGN-BUILD — scroll-driven website demo (concept by NEA Sistemas)

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. No server needed.
Desktop: scroll slowly. Mobile: copy the folder to the phone or serve it on your LAN.

Folders: frames/ (254 WebP, 1600x900), frames-m/ (960x540, used under 760px), stills/ and stills-m/ (9 construction phases), fonts/Inter.ttf (OFL licence).
QA parameter: index.html?sim=0.5 draws the film at 50% without scrolling (used for screenshots).
